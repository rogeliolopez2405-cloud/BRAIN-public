## B. Thread (5 posts)

**1/**

> The AI can be replaced. The BRAIN stays.
>
> Most AI systems keep their continuity in the conversation, the model, the agent or the
> process. When any of those ends, the world ends with it — and a human becomes the memory.

**2/**

> BRAIN separates them. The intelligence is replaceable; the persistent world, its evidence,
> its authority and its verification are not.
>
> Not a model. Not a chatbot. Not MCP. Not an agent framework. It orchestrates nothing and
> calls no provider.

**3/**

> The principle:
>
> Intelligence proposes.
> Evidence establishes.
> Authority decides.
> Reality verifies.
>
> Four steps usually collapsed into one. A model suggesting something isn't evidence. A
> decision isn't a verification — something has to happen and be observed.

**4/**

> What that buys, concretely: a crash leaves a readable intention rather than silence. A
> timeout is recorded as unknown, not as failure. Two worlds that diverge and rejoin don't let
> arrival order decide truth. Conflicts stay conflicts instead of being averaged away.

**5/**

> This is an architecture release, not the implementation. Classification
> GENERAL_ARCHITECTURE_CANDIDATE — it passed my own gate and nobody else's. Validation is
> internal; the repo says so. Five known limitations are published on purpose.
>
> Break it:
> https://github.com/rogeliolopez2405-cloud/BRAIN-public
