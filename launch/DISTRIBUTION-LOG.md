# Distribution log

Which channels actually produce serious technical engagement, recorded rather than remembered.

**Nothing below is filled in.** No post has been published and no metric has been observed.
Every row is added by hand after a real event. **Do not estimate, back-fill or fabricate a
number** — a made-up figure here corrupts the only comparison this file exists to support.

GitHub traffic data (Insights → Traffic) covers the **last 14 days only** and is not retained
beyond that. If it is not written down within two weeks, it is gone.

## Reading this log later

Two questions to ask, in order:

1. **Which channel produced objections?** Not traffic — objections. That is the channel worth
   returning to.
2. **Did any objection change the architecture, the documented limitations, or the
   classification?** If the answer over a whole launch is no, either the material is genuinely
   sound or it did not reach anyone qualified to judge. Traffic numbers cannot tell those two
   apart, and assuming the flattering one is the mistake this file is designed to prevent.
