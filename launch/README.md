## The one rule these all follow

**Ask for falsification, not approval.**

BRAIN is at `GENERAL_ARCHITECTURE_CANDIDATE`. That is a real but modest claim: it passed this
project's own architecture gate and has not been validated anywhere else. Every draft here says
so, states that the validation was produced internally, and says that the public repository
cannot independently reproduce it.

That is not modesty for its own sake. An audience of systems engineers detects overclaiming
immediately, and the first overclaim costs the rest of the argument.

## Claims that must not appear in any of this material

Not production ready · not proven secure · not a universal AI operating system · has not solved
AI memory · not AGI infrastructure · not independently validated · not commercially validated ·
not open source.

If a draft is edited before posting, check it against that list again.

## What is safe to say

- A provider-independent continuity, evidence, authority and verification architecture.
- Classification `GENERAL_ARCHITECTURE_CANDIDATE`, architecture gate 9 of 9.
- A controlled public architecture release — source-available for review, no open-source
  license granted.
- Known limitations are published, five of them, deliberately.
- Validation results are internal and not independently reproduced.
